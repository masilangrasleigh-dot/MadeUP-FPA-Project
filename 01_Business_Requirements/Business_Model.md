## Financial objectives

Management wants to know:

1. Are we meeting budget?
2. Which products are driving revenue?
3. Which regions are underperforming?
4. Where are expenses exceeding budget?
5. What is the latest full-year forecast?
6. What are the largest financial risks?
7. What are the largest opportunities?
8. What actions should management consider?
