# 115. Methodology Document

**Document:** MadeUP FP&A Analytics Project — Methodology & Modeling Framework  
**Scope:** Comprehensive explanation of data generation, historical baselines, planning horizons, budgeting mechanics, forecasting run-rates, and scenario sensitivity analysis.

---

## Data

* **Synthetic data.**  
  All transactional datasets, regional scales, product catalogs, department headcounts, and operating expense records are synthetically generated to simulate an enterprise global technology company environment while protecting proprietary or real-world sensitive information.

---

## Historical period

* **2025.**  
  Full-year historical actual data serving as the baseline for trend analysis, seasonality, cost structures, and year-over-year performance evaluations.

---

## Planning period

* **2026.**  
  The core operating budget and forward-looking projection window spanning the entire 2026 calendar year.

---

## Current-year actual period

* **January–June 2026.**  
  Year-to-Date (YTD) actual performance window used to capture verified financial results, actual sales volumes, operating expenses, and headcount changes.

---

## Forecast period

* **July–December 2026.**  
  Remaining period projection window utilizing run-rate methodologies combined with YTD actuals to establish full-year expected outcomes.

---

## Budget methodology

* **2025 actuals + driver-based growth assumptions.**  
  The 2026 budget does not rely on simple flat-percentage uplifts. Instead, it utilizes a driver-based architecture starting from 2025 actual baselines, incorporating explicit volume and price growth assumptions by business unit (Devices, Cloud & Software, Digital Services), combined with regional economic scale factors and departmental headcount/salary progression models.

---

## Forecast methodology

* **YTD run-rate methodology.**  
  The rolling forecast blends actual results from January to June 2026 with annualized run-rates for July to December 2026, adjusting for identified seasonality trends, fixed operating expenditure commitments, and known run-rate variances.

---

## Scenario methodology

* **Revenue growth sensitivity.**  
  Scenario modeling evaluates financial elasticity across three distinct operating environments:
  * **Downside:** $-3$ percentage point growth adjustment to baseline assumptions, testing margin resilience under top-line compression and fixed Opex rigidity ($\sim \$396.00	ext{M}$ monthly Opex).
  * **Base:** Standard operating plan reflecting baseline volume and pricing growth expectations.
  * **Upside:** $+3$ percentage point growth adjustment, evaluating operating leverage expansion and profitability scaling.
